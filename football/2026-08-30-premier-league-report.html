The familiar attacking pattern is already evident—quick transitions, clinical finishing, and control. Arsenal look ready to challenge for a third consecutive title.

Status: ⚠️ Mixed Start | Record: W1 (3-2 vs Fulham)

However, goalkeeper Robert Sanchez's mistakes allowed Fulham back into the game twice. A win, but concerns about stability at the back must be addressed quickly.

Manchester City

Status: ✅ Positive Start | Record: W1 (2-1 vs Bournemouth)
Manchester City navigated their opening fixture without Pep Guardiola, securing a 2-1 victory over Bournemouth. The transition under new management appears smooth, though it remains early. Their ability to adapt will be crucial in retaining competitiveness.

Manchester United

Status: ❌ Poor Start | Record: L1 (0-2 vs Hull)
Manchester United suffered an embarrassing opening defeat, losing 2-0 at home to Hull City. This is a concerning start that suggests structural issues may persist despite transfer activity.

Tottenham Hotspur
